TIYAMPEPO FOUNDATION LIMITED

Malawi's last mile retention partner for academically promising children especially girls in hard to reach areas

Removing barriers so girls in hard to reach Malawi stay in school and become future leaders

What is Last Mile?

We work where others stop. Usisya in Nkhata Bay North is 4 hours by boat or 8 hours bad road from Mzuzu. Broad ECD and bursary exist but deep retention full package does not. High impact per Kwacha.

Our Proof

  • Beit Trust double classroom block at Usisya Community Day Secondary School - operational
  • 5 scholarships facilitated through FAWEMA for girls to technical colleges and University of Malawi
  • Community initiative since 2022, incorporated 13 May 2026 as Company Limited by Guarantee

What We Do - 3 Pillars - 4 Objectives Only

Pillar 1 - 70% Retention: Keep them in school

Full package: school fees, uniform, self boarding room near school, maize sugar meals, textbooks notebooks, transport, dignity kit, welfare.

Start 2026-2027: 5 students 3 girls 2 boys = 60% girls (50% minimum priority girls)

Criteria: strong academic performance despite poverty, family below poverty line, verifiable home visit, chief letter with stamp phone, good character future leaders.

SOP: Identify, Assess, Recommend, Support, Track. File per child locked. No cash to child - direct to school supplier.

Unit cost: MK150,000 per girl per year keeps her in school.

Pillar 2 - 20% Conducive Environment

Desks MK35k per desk 2 learners per desk 50 desks MK1.75M per school, girls WASH changing room MK2.5M, solar light for study - for schools where our girls learn.

Double classroom block parked to Year 2.

Pillar 3 - 10% Transition

Upkeep K70k per semester plus transport, mentorship, leadership circle, scholarship connection for Form 4 alumni who complete.

ECD Clause - Pipeline Not Program

Foundation does NOT operate ECD centres. ECD is pipeline for future retention cohort. Support limited to readiness and conducive environment in feeder centres. Detailed activities defined in Strategic Plan and SOP, not in Constitution.

Verification and Safeguarding

  • No ghost beneficiary - academic report, home visit 2 persons one female for girls daylight chief knowledge, chief letter with stamp phone
  • Child safeguarding zero tolerance - file per child locked, photo consent parent guardian child 13+ chief witness, first name only public
  • Gender: 50% minimum girls target 60% 3 girls 2 boys, boys as allies for mindset change
  • Stewardship: finance register open for audit, resource centre income MK200k per month funds 16 girls per year
